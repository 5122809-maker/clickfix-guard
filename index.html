How a ClickFix attack works

  1. LureA hacked site, fake ad or search result shows a fake CAPTCHA or “fix this error” box.
  2. Silent copyThe page’s JavaScript copies a hidden malicious command to your clipboard.
  3. You run itYou are told to press Win+R (or open Terminal), paste and press Enter.
  4. PayloadThe command downloads an infostealer or remote-access trojan. Antivirus sees a user-launched command.

If you already ran one

  • Disconnect from the network (Wi-Fi off, cable out) and tell your IT team or teacher.
  • From a different, clean device, change passwords and sign out all sessions for email, banking and social accounts.
  • Run a full scan with Microsoft Defender or your security product, or reinstall the OS if unsure.
  • Rule of thumb: no real CAPTCHA ever asks you to open Run, PowerShell or Terminal.
